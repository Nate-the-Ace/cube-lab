The unit is the match, not the game: a round is a best-of-three, so a round lost 0-2 is one loss here, not two. A bye is recorded but never counted — it isn't a match anyone played, so including it would quietly reward not playing. Every rate below divides by matches actually played. Score counts a draw as half a win, the usual Swiss convention; Win% counts only outright wins.
